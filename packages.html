Packages and How to Pay

Now that you've seen what Castle Respite has to offer, you're ready to plan your vacation. You can reserve any days on the calendar, from as little as three days all the way up to ten days. The more people you have with you on your vacation, the less money you will spend per night. That's why we encourage you to bring no more than twenty-five in your group for maximum quality time and maximum discount.

Once you've decided on how long you want to stay in the castle and how many people you want to take with you, you can find our reservation form located underneath the planning and packaging list to reserve a time for you and your family. When you've reserved your spot, we'll reach out to you in order to complete your transaction. After that, you're all set! Whether it begins next week or next month, you'll have signed up for a memorable medieval experience you won't be forgetting any time soon.

We hope you'll enjoy your stay.
