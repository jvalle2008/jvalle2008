Stables

One of the best features of your bed and breakfast castle is that it is entirely pet free. We do not allow you to keep your pets in the cellar, pantry, or master bedrooms, so we have a special place for your animal buddy. With plenty of space and a surplus of pet food, our stables are a prime place for your dog or cat. Maybe not your hamster or pet fish. Inside our stables are:
  • Four stalls that can hold at least twenty medium-sized dogs per stall. We once hosted a dog show in our courtyard.
  • Ten bags of dog food and cat food.
  • Cushony mattresses for your pets.
  • The mattresses in the stables were previously in the master bedrooms. One family had two dobermans who tore up the beds. We've never allowed pets in the master bedrooms since.
  • It is possible for people to sleep with their pets in the stable, but we would not recommend it since the shower situation is worse than the toilet situation.
