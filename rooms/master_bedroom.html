Master Bedroom

The Master Bedrooms are two of our most exquisite rooms in the castle. Here, you can rest after a long day of fun activities in Branson, which you can see from up high in your balcony view. Let's take a look at what you're in store for:
  • Kick back and relax on your very own King-Sized Bed.
  • Highly durable, yet soft mattress.
  • 700 Thread-Count Sheets.
  • Curtains on top of bed frame, allowing you to shut out the light from inside and outside your room.
  • Fireplace beside bed provides warmth during the winter.
  • 65 Inch TV sitting in front of your bed, providing late-night entertainment.
  • Over 200 Channels to choose from, including all Disney channels, Cartoon Network, Boomerang, Comedy Central, and more.
  • Two-seater couch sits between bed and TV, allowing you to get a closer look at the screen.
  • Bluetooth speakers can either be connected to the TV during movie night, or to your phone to play your favorite songs.
  • Two nightstands complete with lamps and alarm clocks beside the bed.
