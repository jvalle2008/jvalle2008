Pantry

Connected to the Cellar, the Pantry holds the rest of the foods, such as grains, cheese, butter, fruits, and vegetables. Here, you can slice bread, chop carrots, and season meat. Additionally, we have an assortment of board games you can take up to the Great Hall to spend quality time with the family and get to work on bonding. The things you'll find in the pantry are:
  • Bread, Butter, Fruits, Vegetables, and Spices.
  • Wooden bowls and cutting boards
  • Various board games for tabletop entertainment
  • Our games include Monopoly, Risk, Uno, Phase 10, and Diplomacy. The friendliest games we could find.
