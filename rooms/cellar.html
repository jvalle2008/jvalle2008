Cellar

Going from the Great Hall's left doorway, down a spiral set of stairs, you'll find your brand new refrigerator and kitchen! Here, we have several barrels filled to the brim with beer, wine, Coke, Pepsi, chicken, pork, and beef. Included with food, we have the appliances and utensils to cook it too. Here is what you're getting:
  • Barrels of beverages on the left side of the room when you reach the bottom of the stairs.
  • Each barrel is labeled based on beverage.
  • Barrels of meat on the right side of the room.
  • Each barrel is labeled based on meat.
  • Microwave, Oven, Pots, Pans, Knives, Utensils, and Barbeque grill.
