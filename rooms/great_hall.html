Great Hall

The Great Hall is where you will be eating all of your meals inside the castle. It is the biggest room in your temporary medieval home, and can be shared by over twenty people. For an ideal experience, we recommend bringing at at least fifteen people with you. The sounds of several people eating and talking and having fun will make you feel alive in that one room more than anywhere else on earth. Here are just some of the things waiting for your arrival:
  • The Royal Table is set for the head of the family, and can seat up to seven people.
  • Table setting includes tablecloth, silver bowls, silver silverware, and ornate chairs.
  • The family that bought the b&b are allowed to sit at this table. Additionally, the are allowed to choose who will sit with them if there is room.
  • The Servants' Tables were once meant for servants and guests, and can seat up to sixteen people per table.
  • Table setting includes wooden bowls, wooden silverware, and stools.
  • These tables are for the children, younger siblings, or in-laws.

Whether you cook from home or bring back takeout, nothing is better than holding a feast in the Great Hall with all of your friends and family!
