Privy

Out of all the rooms in the castle, the Privy is probably the most uninteresting room. However, an indoor bathroom was the biggest luxury imaginable in Medieval times. Now, with modern plumbing, it isn't that impressive to you, but at least the leg-room is nice. Let's see what you've got:
  • Three-feet of leg room
  • Modern plumbing directly to sewers. This vacation would stink if you had to shovel it out yourself, and throwing poop in the streets is illegal now.
  • Wooden door, locks from the inside and provides a small amount of privacy.
  • A smell that reeks of medieval times.
